RTFx is audio duration divided by processing time: values below one are slower
than real time. Cold wall time was about 19 minutes 35 seconds and warm wall time
about 13 minutes 41 seconds for 6 minutes 41 seconds of audio. Both runs were
slower than real time; this does not establish a home-server speed estimate.

The measurement process was started before the final source-timeline hardening;
inference parameters and model were unchanged. Its transcript metrics exclude
final metadata writing and rename; caller wall time covers the entire call.
`stt benchmark` reports full caller `wall_seconds` and `rtfx_total` explicitly.
Peak RAM samples process RSS every 50 ms; this can miss short spikes and includes
the whole process, not just model weights.
